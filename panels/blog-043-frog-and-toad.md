We've read a lot of books with the kiddo over the last few years, but Lobel's
**Frog and Toad** short stories from the 70s are my (and his) favorites right
now.

There are 20 short stories across 4 books. I originally wanted to rank them from
my favorite to least favorite in this post, but I couldn't agree with any
ordering that I wrote. Each story brings something great and different, from the
relationships between Frog and Toad, the little things they teach, the humor,
the joy, the despair.

So, instead of a ranking, I've written a short tldr of each story for myself, so
I can quickly look up which is which:

### Frog and Toad Are Friends

- **Spring.** Toad doesn't want to get out of bed. Frog does.
- **The Story.** Frog doesn't feel well. Toad tries to think of a story.
- **A Lost Button.** Toad drags Frog around to find a button. Toad finds it.
- **A Swim.** Toad doesn't want anyone to see his bathing suit. They do.
- **The Letter.** Frog writes a letter to toad. It takes an accurate amount of
  time.

### Frog and Toad Together

- **A List.** Toad makes a list of things to do. He doesn't.
- **The Garden.** Toad helps his garden grow. It's hard.
- **Cookies.** An exercise in will power.
- **Dragons and Giants.** An exercise in bravery.
- **The Dream.** Toad's truly unsettling dream performing feats on stage.

### Frog and Toad All Year

- **Down the Hill.** Winter. Frog and Toad go sledding. Then Toad goes sledding.
- **The Corner.** Spring. Frog reminisces about finding Spring around the
  corner.
- **Ice Cream.** Summer. Toad tries to bring Frog back some ice cream.
- **The Surprise.** Fall. Frog and Toad rake each other's leaves.
- **Christmas Eve.** Winter. Frog runs late and Toad worries.

### Days With Frog and Toad

- **Tomorrow.** Toad doesn't want to do chores. So he does chores.
- **The Kite.** Frog and Toad fly a kite.
- **Shivers.** Frog tells a scary story. Did that really happen?
- **The Hat.** Frog gets Toad a birthday gift. Thinks big thoughts.
- **Alone.** Frog wants to be alone. Toad worries.
