# Guild Wars 1

I played the original GW1 back in high school, I still have my 2 install discs
in that CD binder every 30-something has, sitting next two my 2 Lord of the
Rings Online discs from around the same time.

And like 7 discs for Neverwinter Nights 2.

And one for FFXIV: A Realm Reborn. Although fun story there. By 2013, it was
pretty easy to just download games, but they closed off new sales of the game
for the ffxiv arr launch due to unexpected high demand. So I ended up driving
like ~20 miles to the nearest Best Buy that had a physical copy to play the
game. Although my character
[Zk Hr](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/lodestone/character/3576874/) created on
August 30, 2013 a few day after launch isn't my main anymore. I switched to
[Zakhar Iceteno](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/lodestone/character/25668161/)
when I started playing again in 2019.

But yeah, I remember the original guild wars was a lot of fun when it came to
trying out weird and interesting builds all in the name of getting as many globs
of ectoplasm as you possibly could. So monks with hardly any hp, rangers that
queue up a bunch of traps to go off all at once, etc.

# Guild Wars 2

After playing the GW2 betas before launch, I'd made a plan to build a bunch of
the 20-item storage chests to sell to other players that just wanted more
storage and make lots of gold/gems early on. But that didn't go to plan, since
the market for selling stuff was broken at launch. ¯\\\_(ツ)\_/¯

Coming back to GW2 after a many year hiatus, I really appreciate how the game
supports that MMO vibe where you can just hang out and do stuff. I'll go to an
area planning get some resources for crafting, end up trying to complete some
hearts, find myself doing some big global boss with a bunch of players that
showed up, and then realize I can finish up some achievement I'd been working
towards.

But that does mean that I'm terrible at sticking to the main quest. I finally
finished my story, picking up after a decade right after somebody died whose
memory we would never forget... and I had no idea who they were. Also finished
living world season 1, but I'm still in the middle of season 2. Haven't even
made it to Heart of Thorns yet.

# Guild Wars 3

After playing GW2 for a little bit in May, I decided to support the devs on May
28th and pick up the collection with the first few expansions. So of course, GW3
is announced a few days later on June 5th and everything goes on sale... you're
welcome everyone!

I'm definitely hyped to try out the public betas next year and excited to see
what comes next.
